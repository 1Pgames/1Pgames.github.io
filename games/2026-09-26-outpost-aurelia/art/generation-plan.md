# Outpost Aurelia — bulk generation plan (for the orchestrator's fan-out)

Style lock: `art/style.json` (`aurelia-retro-nasa`, 18-colour palette, 1 vision anchor).
Manifest: `art/manifest.json`, generated by `python3 art/tools/build-manifest.py` (edit the script,
not the JSON; it syncs grid/cellSize/duration/loop from every shipped `sprite-metadata.json`). Lint:
`python3 "$(git rev-parse --show-toplevel)/.claude/skills/game-art/references/manifest-lint.py" art/manifest.json`
exits 0 with 0 errors and 0 warnings (18 groups, 167 assets, 35 qcExceptions after consolidation; the
template arena groups are gone). Set gate: `python3 art/tools/figure-ground-cast.py` (4 biomes × day/night,
full cast). Integrator contract: `art/wiring.md`.

## 0. What every generation agent must know (measured in the anchor pass)

1. **The provider has no alpha.** Every sprite goes on a flat magenta key and is keyed out. xai
   does NOT return #FF00FF. It returns hot pink, measured on 6 of 6 anchor raws at
   `rgb(252, 45-80, 169-183)`, and the reinforced "pure RGB(255,0,255), not pink" wording did
   not change that (raws after the rewording: (252,45,175), (252,63,183), (252,74,172),
   (252,61,175)). Pink sits 113 from magenta, outside the hd-* default threshold 105, so the first
   exports failed `background-contamination`. **Fix (processing, not prompt): `"threshold": 150`
   in every sprite marker.** All 18 palette colours measure ≥ 207 from magenta (softLimit 150+55
   = 205), so nothing in the palette is at risk. **Erratum (buildings-c, measured):** the key also
   comes back as `rgb(252, 84-95, 171-183)`; on input-bearing calls the chroma guard then protects that
   pinker key and the export fails strict `source-edge-touch`. Process such raws WITHOUT the guard
   (`rekey.py` / the group `process_raw.py`), never by raising the threshold.
2. **Run `python3 art/tools/rekey.py <asset dir>` after every accepted sprite export.** With
   `styleProfile` bound, the sprite-forge chroma guard keeps every keyable pixel that sits nearer
   ANY palette anchor than pure magenta. On pink raws that means the antialiased pink rim and every
   glow halo ship fully opaque. Measured on icons-goods-a: 13,466 pinkish px guarded against 198
   re-keyed. The script re-runs the processor with the export's own effective params minus the
   guard, and keeps the marker's metadata as `sprite-metadata.marker.json`. (Filed through
   `xd://report_issue`.) Keep `styleProfile` in the marker: it carries the prompt merge, the
   vision anchor and the audit record.
3. **Buildings are processed with `scale fit 0.9, align bottom`, not hd-body `preserve`.** preserve
   kept the provider's size: the drill came out at 130/256 px height, too small to read at 128 px.
   Pass `"scale":"fit","fit":0.9,"align":"bottom","componentMode":"all"` in the marker. **Erratum
   (buildings-b/-c, fauna-a, colony-motion, measured):** the marker key is `scale`, NOT `scaleMode` —
   `scaleMode` is silently dropped (`extensions/sprite-generate.ts:26,694`) and the export comes back
   `scaleMode: "preserve"`, `componentMode: "largest"` with no override recorded. Verify
   `metadata.output.scaleMode == "fit"` on every export. Fit uses one shared sheet scale (bodyScaleCv 0.000 measured).
4. **Full-bleed tiles:** text-only (no `styleProfile` in the marker, so the anchor is not appended),
   OR an accepted sibling tile as explicit Image 1. Marker `fullBleed:true, threshold:1,
   edgeThreshold:1, cellSize:512`. xai welds a pink frame around every swatch (85-90 px on the 2
   anchor raws). **Erratum (terrain, measured):** this wave's frames were 137-250 px, and `deframe.py`
   crashes on any frame wider than ~113 px (its scan lines sit inside the band) and misses darker frames
   like `rgb(205,42,118)`; strip with `art/exports/terrain/deframe_bbox.py` (per-row/column measurement)
   instead. Then check L* 18-32 and run figure-ground against the full cast.
   Full-bleed tiles are FIELDS in figure-ground, never actors.
5. **Actor calls** (buildings, fauna, props, icons, fx, relics, deposits, cover) put
   `"styleProfile":"games/2026-09-26-outpost-aurelia/art/style.json"` in the marker. The middleware
   APPENDS `art/refs/vision-1.png` AFTER any explicit `input` (`extensions/sprite-generate.ts:860`,
   `merged.input = [...existing, ...anchors]`), so do NOT also list it in `input`. With no explicit input
   the anchor is Image 1; open `subject` with the fixing clause:
   `Image 1 fixes the rendering style, palette, lighting, outline and finish - match it exactly. Draw a NEW subject in that style: <subject>. Do not copy Image 1's composition or objects.`
   **Erratum (buildings-a, measured — input order):** siblings (Mk III, apex, fauna attack) that pass the
   accepted base as explicit `input` make THAT the **Image 1** and the vision anchor **Image 2**. Write
   "Image 1 fixes this exact building's identity; Image 2 fixes the rendering style…", and pass a single
   frame (frame 0 flattened on #FF00FF), never the whole sheet — a whole-sheet Image 1 made xai weld grid
   dividers on 3/3 calls (buildings-b) and near-copy the base (apex).
6. **Anchors already accepted** are staged in `art/anchors/<group>/<id>/`. The group owner copies
   them to `public/assets/generated/<group>/<id>/` **when the whole group lands**. A present group
   directory with a missing asset makes `gen-art-registry.mjs` throw.
7. Retry budget: 2 regenerations per asset per symptom. Increment `attempts` in
   `art/tools/build-manifest.py` → `ATTEMPTS`, and write `qcExceptions[]` before any 3rd attempt.

## 1. Prompt skeleton (actor, NxN sheet)

```json
{
  "subject": "Image 1 fixes the rendering style, palette, lighting, outline and finish - match it exactly. Draw a NEW subject in that style: <WHAT IT IS, material, 1 silhouette trait that differs from its siblings>. <frame list if animated>. Do not copy Image 1's composition or objects.",
  "composition": "<R>x<C> grid, cells in reading order, one subject centred per cell at identical size and base line, subject spans about 65% of the cell width, entirely inside the central safe area, nothing crosses a cell boundary, no divider lines; frontal 3/4 top-down orthographic view (top + front face ~30% of height)",
  "style": "thick 3-4px dark ink outline around the whole silhouette, no glow halo outside the outline",
  "scene": "background is one flat fill of pure chroma-key magenta RGB(255,0,255) #FF00FF in every cell and directly beneath the subject; no ground plane, no shadow, no text",
  "aspect_ratio": "1:1", "image_size": "1024x1024",
  "changes": ["OMP_SPRITE_EXPORT:{\"outputDir\":\"games/2026-09-26-outpost-aurelia/public/assets/generated/<group>/<id>\",\"rows\":R,\"cols\":C,\"profile\":\"hd-body|hd-fx\",\"cellSize\":256,\"duration\":<ms or 0>,\"threshold\":150,\"styleProfile\":\"games/2026-09-26-outpost-aurelia/art/style.json\"}"]
}
```

Colour law for prompts: colony = cream hull, sodium amber and teal. Aurelia = violet, ochre and
nacre. Fauna = glassy violet-grey chitin with pale lime lens eyes. **Never paint red or green
outlines**: the fauna outline `#3a1712` is baked at runtime.
